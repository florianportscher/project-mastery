# Tutoring scenarios on Muse Ember, 2026-11-14

Issue #302: the instructions now give a session a shape (opening, blocks, final check, time, plain words). The eleven scenarios in `evals/tutor/scenarios/` ran on `meta/muse-ember` through `pnpm tutor-eval`, on the gold course `linear-algebra-I`. Three are new whole-session scenarios (01, 10, 11) with an eight-item rubric. For each run, a Claude Sonnet subagent played the Student and scored each checklist item 0 to 2 (2 met, 1 partly or late, 0 not met). Subagents ran one at a time, each scenario once per round. Round 1 ran all eleven on `c674ce6`, and round 2 ran the nine with an item at 0 on `a1aa18b`. Then the rounds stopped, as the spec says.

## Changes and their reasons

**Round 1 → round 2 (`a1aa18b`)**, each against a general problem, not a scenario's wording:

- **Tasks and readings were announced in text, without `ask` or `showReading`.** This happened in 04, 06, 01 and 03, for example "Now try it on fresh numbers:" and then nothing. 06 opened with a plan and "Read the script, pp. 35–36, now" but no reading. The opening rule's "Only then explain or ask" read as "not in this reply". Changes: the opening now starts the plan's first step in the same reply, and a block's task is the prompt of an `ask` in the reply that explains it.
- **The opening ran when the Student asked something else** (05), and it was skipped when the Student said "Let's start." (10). The opening is now once per session, a few short lines, one line when the first message asks for something else, and it puts due Reviews first.
- **Standing was given in app terms**, such as "0 of 7" and "your last session on 2026-11-12". The AI also explained its own process ("I asked the final check again because…"). Where the Student stands is now said as what they can do, never as counts or dates, and the Words rule forbids explaining why it asks what it asks.
- **The question over a Topic was graded with help `some`**, so every KC stayed "not yet shown" and the final check asked all of them again (10). Help `none` is now defined as a question asked before explaining any of its KCs. The final check now excludes KCs shown without help today.
- **A claim the Student brought got a lecture** with the counterexample (08), as if it were a new Topic. A claim is now answered by the rule for wrong claims, not with a lecture.

## What stays open

- **Announced but not asked.** This went down but did not go away: 04 and 08 in round 2 still ended replies on "Try applying all three:" with no `ask`. It is the strongest failure of the shape on Muse Ember. #277 saw it in about one run in five, and longer explanations make it more frequent. Code that checks for it is ruled out (#253 and this spec). If it stays, the next step the spec names is a lesson prepared per Topic at the map build.
- **Help labels.** `none` and `some` were mixed up in most runs (01, 03, 10, 11). The question over a Topic still came back as `some` in 10, so the final check repeated KCs already shown.
- **Due Reviews next to focused work.** 07 asked no Review in either round, as in every round of #277.
- **Exam scope from the script alone** (05, both rounds). It says "not on this exam" without reading the exam information.
- **The final check** covered only graded KCs, not KCs explained but never tasked (01). This follows from blocks that skip their task.
- **Narration** ("Grading your answer first…") is in most runs, as before.
- **Time.** No whole-session run reached the end of its length: 10 ended after about 4 minutes, and 11 answered "How long?" with 4 minutes when about 6 had passed. The "about 5 minutes left" rule is untested.
- **Blocks of one KC** (01): after "that's a lot", it split the tasks into one-line asks, 7 in 30 minutes against the limit of 6.

## Notes

- **Spend, from `ai_usage` before each `end`:** 149 model calls and **$0.53** over 20 runs (round 1: 84 calls, $0.29; round 2: 65 calls, $0.25). Every run's Student was deleted.
- **429s.** Two came (01 and 06, round 2). One came after the reply had called `closeQuestion`, so `retryRateLimited` did not retry, as designed. The next message got "already graded" from the tool.
- **`compute` refusals.** These are still common for matrices named `A`, `B`, `J` or `S`, which are mathjs units. Each was fixed on the retry.
- **`ask` refusals.** KC ids from another Topic's lookup were refused twice (06, 07). The Student saw no question until they complained.
- **Untested.** The scenario 11 move "I haven't seen this before" never fired, because every idea had been explained before it was asked.
