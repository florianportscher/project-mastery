# Vision

> **The AI handles everything about learning except the learning itself.**

## Mission

Project Mastery gets students ready for their exams. They always know what matters, what to do today and how ready they are. Their study hours go into real understanding, so they walk in calm and the grade confirms it.

## The problem

Students lose much of their study time to the bureaucracy of learning: finding what matters across slides, scripts and old exams, guessing what the examiner cares about, making a plan and sticking to it. Much of the remaining time goes into methods that feel productive but don't stick, such as rereading, highlighting and cramming. The strategies that work (spaced retrieval, interleaving, active problem solving) are well known, but hard to sustain alone against human nature.

Most students already study with a general AI chatbot. It explains anything at any hour. It also hands out solutions, forgets last week, does not know the exam and says yes when asked "am I ready?". It made the easy part of studying easier and left the hard parts where they were: knowing what matters, doing the work yourself, coming back tomorrow, and knowing honestly where you stand.

This is what takes the fun out of studying: hours of effort with little visible progress, the feeling that everything slips away again, and growing anxiety as the exam gets closer. Studying isn't inherently boring. Inefficient studying is.

## How it works

A student gets something useful in the first five minutes, at any point of the semester: stuck on this week's exercise sheet, three weeks before the exam, or two days before it.

1. **Upload**: The student uploads course materials: slides, scripts, exercises, old exams.
2. **Brief**: They tell the AI about the exam: date, format, what the lecturer emphasizes, what to skip, their goals and their current level. The Brief asks as it goes and blocks nothing.
3. **Plan**: The AI analyzes the materials and old exams, works out what matters most, and builds a learning plan that follows the exam date, the goal and the student's preferences. The plan fits the hours the student has and says honestly what it leaves out.
4. **Learn**: The AI tutors the student through the plan, session by session, and keeps adapting it to what the student has mastered, struggles with or wants.
5. **Ready**: Before the exam, the student practices on realistic mock exams and knows honestly how ready they are, topic by topic.

## Principles

1. **The student does the thinking.** The AI explains enough for the student's own work to start, then hands it over: retrieval, problem solving, self-explanation. Explanations unlock the student's own work; they never replace it.
2. **Learning science by default.** Spaced retrieval, interleaving, worked examples that fade into independent practice, feedback on the reasoning and not just the final answer. The student never needs to know these terms; the app simply works this way.
3. **The exam is the goal; lasting knowledge is how we get there.** The methods that hold up in the exam are the ones that last. The plan always fits the time that is left: with weeks to go it builds and spaces, with days to go it sorts by exam relevance × weakest mastery.
4. **Grounded in the course.** The uploaded materials are the primary source, cited down to the slide or page. Questions that go deeper or beyond them are welcome and get answered, clearly marked as going beyond the course.
5. **A tutor, not a supervisor.** The AI recommends what to do next. The student stays in control and can skip, reorder or redirect, and the plan adapts strongly to them.
6. **Honest, not paternalistic.** Solutions are not locked away. The AI encourages the student's own attempt first and helps step by step. If the student takes shortcuts, the app makes it visible that no learning happened. Readiness is shown as evidence, what the student has solved alone, until enough real exam results exist to back a forecast.
7. **The reward is understanding.** Learning is fun when it works. Motivation comes from the moment a confusing topic becomes clear, from knowledge that stays, and from progress made visible. Points and streaks for their own sake are not the goal.
8. **Zero bureaucracy for the student.** Everything that isn't learning is the AI's job: finding content, analyzing old exams, planning, scheduling reviews. The app speaks the student's words and never asks them to learn its own.
9. **The app helps the student show up.** The student sets when they study. The app reminds them at those times, keeps sessions short with a clear end, and makes starting easy.
10. **Right, or it says it isn't sure.** A tutor that is confidently wrong is worse than none. Results are checked by code wherever they can be, generated problems are verified before a student sees them, and the app says so when it cannot check.
11. **It works where the student works.** Maths happens on paper. The student solves by hand and shows a photo, on the phone they have with them.

## Who it's for

- **MVP:** University students in math and STEM subjects, preparing for written exams. The interface is English; the tutoring is in the language of the course's materials from the first day.
- **Later:** more subjects and exam formats, K-12 students, and interfaces in other languages.

The MVP is built so it can expand in these directions, but none of this is built now.

## Non-goals

- **Never:** a homework or exam solver whose purpose is to skip the learning.
- **Not in the MVP:**
  - an open-ended general chatbot
  - note-taking or document management
  - tools for teachers or schools
  - social or collaborative features
  - users who are minors

## Built to be sustainable

Project Mastery should be cheap to run and light enough to use every day.

- Quality comes first where the student feels it: the tutor's replies use a model good enough that a session beats a general chatbot. Everything the student never sees uses the cheapest model that works, and each upload is processed once and reused.
- Every feature is designed with its cost per student in mind. Usage is metered and limited per account, so costs stay visible and heavy use cannot run away unnoticed.
- Who pays stays open until students have shown they want the app; until then it is free for a limited number of testers.

## What success looks like

**First exam period**

- A new student is in a useful session within ten minutes of the first upload.
- Students come back for a third session without being asked.
- After the exam, most testers would be disappointed to lose the app and can say what it did that their chatbot did not.
- They use it for the next exam.

**Later**

- The readiness estimate matches real results.
- Grades improve.
