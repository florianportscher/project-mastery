# Questions as first-class objects

> Amended by #448: a Question is a task in parts. The owner-only table `question_parts` (`question_id`, `position` 1 to 6, `prompt`, `kc_id`, `form` `short`, `long` or `choice`, `options` 2 to 4 for `choice`, the Student's `answer_text` and `choice`, `result` `full`, `partial`, `wrong` or `unanswered`, and a `note` of at most 300 characters) has RLS as `attempts`: the Student reads their own, adds parts only on their own Question for a KC it tests, and updates only the answer, grade and help columns (`answer_text`, `choice`, `result`, `note`, and from #449 `hints` and `solution_shown_at`, which the function `record_question_move` sets in one statement when the Student presses Hint or Show the solution). `chat_messages.answer` holds a Submit's answers, and from #449 `chat_messages.move` a press. An Attempt's `help` is no longer one value of the Question: it is the most help among the KC's parts, from their presses (a shown solution is `solution_shown`, a hint at least `some`) or what `grade` says. The Student's answer is what they submit, or a message with a photo; typed messages no longer join `answer_text`. Attempts keep their meaning, one per KC per Question: the worst answered part of that KC, none for a KC with only `unanswered` parts. A Question without parts (Mock exams, earlier Chat Questions) renders and grades as before, with no backfill.

> Amended by [ADR 0028](0028-one-study-session.md), which supersedes its session parts: one kind of Study session, no `kind` on Questions or Attempts ([ADR 0027](0027-per-day-scheduling-and-one-ranking.md)).

**Context.** Until M1 (#173) the AI recorded evidence with one `recordAttempt` call per problem: a correct-or-not flag per KC, written after the fact and tied to nothing in the Chat. Nothing linked an attempt to the question that was asked or the answer that earned it, so grading could not be checked, a numeric answer could not be compared with the Exercise's key, and later modes (review, practice, mock exams) had no session to belong to.

**Decision.** Three owner-only tables replace the old log. `study_sessions` holds one row per Chat (`chat_id` unique), started by a database trigger when the Chat is inserted, with its Course and `started_at`/`ended_at`; a Mock exam's session has no Chat. Since #248 there is one kind of session: the modes, Topic and stages it once had are gone, and `closed_at` marks the close call's store. A Student may update only `rating` (1 to 5), `rating_note` (at most 300 characters) and `ended_at` on their own session. `questions` holds each graded question the AI asks in a session: its Markdown `prompt`, optional Exercise or Problem, the Student's answer (`answer_text`, `answer_message_id`) and when it was asked, answered and graded; its KCs are in `question_kcs`. The Student's messages after a question, until it is graded, are its answer; a mock task is the Question its `mock_items` row points at. `attempts` is recreated as the grade of one question on one of its KCs: `result` (`full`, `partial`, `wrong`), `help`, and `check` (`match`, `mismatch`, `unchecked` or null) for the answer comparison. Row Level Security lets a Student add attempts only on KCs their own question tests, in that question's session, and never change one. A deleted Chat leaves its session (`chat_id` set null), so the learning evidence survives; a deleted Course takes everything. A Course's attempts are read through their session's `course_id`. The review ladder read `full` as correct and `partial` and `wrong` as incorrect until FSRS replaced it in M2; ADR 0019 supersedes that reading with FSRS ratings. Since M7b (#186) the `gradeMock` workflow grades a submitted Mock exam with the service role: each `mock_items` row gets `points_awarded` (half points), `steps`, `check` and `graded_at` (set without points when grading failed), each of its KCs an attempt in the mock's session, and the mock its `score`, `brier`, `grading_started_at` and `graded_at`; the Student never writes these columns. `exam_results` holds the Student's grade in the real exam, unique per Course and exam date, insert-only, a null grade when skipped.

**Consequences.** The migration deletes every Chat and attempt, which were test data, and drops `chats.model_choice` with the Chat's model picker: the Chat runs on one model. Reads of a Course's attempts join `study_sessions`. Grading, the numeric check and evals can now point at a stored question and answer. Exercises gain `final_answers` for the numeric check; those analysed before stay null until their map is rebuilt.
