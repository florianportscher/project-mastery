# Topics from KCs, parse without Outline

> Supersedes [ADR 0023](0023-knowledge-map-from-chapters.md) where it makes an Outline of Chapters, extracts per Chapter unit and builds Topics from Chapters. The rest of 0023 stands (reasoning on build calls, call timeouts, split and given-up units, the tree call made twice).

**Context.** A Topic covered whole Chapters, and the Chapters were each Material's own structure. Slides with one Chapter per slide, or a deeply nested script, gave dozens of Topics with one or two KCs, and how a Material was laid out decided the map. The Outline call also cost a call per Material, and its Chapters leaked into units, labels and the Brief (#351).

**Decision.**

- **The Topic tree is built from KCs.** One call (task `topic`, reasoning high) sees every live KC in Course order (short id, name, the Material and heading of its first source), the Course language and the stored Topics with their KC ids. It answers a flat list: level 1 or 2, name, KC ids, central question and `continues`. The prompt asks for Topics that are each one subject learned as a block, usually a few to about ten KCs, joining what several Materials teach; a level-1 Topic with children only groups them. There is no limit in code on a Topic's size.
- **Code places every KC** (`planTopicTree`): each KC goes to the first Topic naming it; a KC no Topic names joins the Topic of the KC before it in Course order (the first, the one after it); deeper levels become level 2; Topics left empty go. KCs from the exercise call are placed like any other, so `loadKcVectors` and the exercise-KC placement rules go.
- **Continuity** by `continues`, then by the free Topic sharing most KCs with the stored one. The tree hash covers KC ids, names and the language. `topics.chapter_ids` goes.
- **The parse has no Outline call.** `chapters` goes. A heading unit starts the next chunk and becomes its `heading` (`chunks.heading`); a chunk before any heading has none, and one after it keeps the previous one. The page read answers each batch's language and unusual notation, and the parse fills `materials.language` and `materials.notes` (at most 1,000 characters) from those answers. The page read also gets the Material's name, and one skip reason, `nothing_to_learn`, covers every page with nothing to learn or practise, so such pages are not transcribed.
- **Teaching batches replace Chapter units.** A Material's `content` chunks in reading order are packed up to `UNIT_CHARS`; a batch that is half full ends before a chunk under a new heading. Extractions (`unit_extractions`) are keyed by Material, kind and batch. Hashing, retries and splitting stay. The extraction prompt sizes a KC as one exam task, whatever the Material: an overview, aside, example or remark belongs to the KC it serves.
- **The check report** lists Material pages with content that no KC cites, in place of Chapters without KCs. An Exercise's label reads "heading · Material p. N".
- **Shipping.** One migration gives existing chunks a heading from their Chapter title, then drops `chapters`, `chapter_ids` and the Chapter references, and marks every map as needing Update map.

**Consequences.** The map no longer depends on how a Material is laid out: slides, a script and a sheet that teach one subject meet in one Topic. Calls grow with the text, not with Chapters, and a Course needs no call for structure. Headings are only hints, so a Material with no headings gives chunks without them and Exercise labels without a heading. Topic identity rests on the tree call and on shared KCs: a Topic loses its notes and choices when no new Topic shares a KC with it. Because KC size and Topic size are guidance in prompts, a model that ignores them gives an uneven tree, which a smoke run on real Materials checks, not code.
