# Gap analysis, 2026-11-27

The app compared with [good-tutoring.md](../features/good-tutoring.md): each principle and moment, what the app does today, the evidence from archived Chat cases, a verdict, and the gaps ranked and split into rounds (#436). Tutoring improves round by round, with a test by the maintainer between rounds.

**Sources.** The code: the instructions (`study/ai/instructions.ts`), the tools (`study/ai/tools.ts`), the tutor context and the close call as [tutoring.md](../features/tutoring.md) describes them, and the Chat's components. The cases: one archived Chat, `2026-11-25-chat-1`, a session planned for 45 minutes that ran 90, on business-finance slides in German, cited by call id. The open `observation` issues #417 to #422, and #380 from the map build. One case is thin evidence: a verdict that rests on code alone says so.

**Verdicts.** _Met_: the instructions or code do what the principle asks, and the case does not contradict it. _Partly_: some of it, or the instructions ask for it and the case shows it failing. _Missing_: nothing asks for it. _Contradicted_: a line or a feature asks for the opposite.

## Gaps, ranked

**Why round 1 is these.** G1 and G2 are what several other principles build on: per-part verdicts make GT-5's first error and GT-6's fix-at-once possible, and Hint (G3) needs a part to give the next rung for. G3 and G4 are the buttons the standard settles beside the answer form. G5 to G9 are lines of the instructions that ask for the opposite of the standard, and each costs a sentence. G10 and G12 are as cheap and G10 rests on A evidence, but they add behaviour rather than remove a contradiction; they lead round 2, so the test of round 1 shows what the answer forms and the rewritten lines did. G14 to G16 wait as the issue planned: the evidence does not rank them above round 1. G4 is low on learning but closes an observation for a line's cost.

**Outside the standard.** #420 (the session's language taken from the app's English start text) breaks no principle: it stays an observation.
